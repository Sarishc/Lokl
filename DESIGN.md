# Lokl Design System

Lokl should feel like a fast neighbourhood market in your pocket: warm, compact, trustworthy, and built for one-handed use while moving through an Indian street or commute.

## Tokens

- `--color-background` / `--color-ink`: roasted black-brown base, chosen to keep the dark theme but move away from generic blue-black SaaS.
- `--color-surface` / `--color-surface-strong`: warmer raised surfaces inspired by shop shutters and evening market stalls.
- `--color-primary`: marigold ticket yellow for price, primary actions, and active state; it gives listing value a recognizable Lokl signal.
- `--color-secondary`: teal trust accent for verification, distance, and location cues.
- `--color-text-primary` / `--color-text-muted`: warm off-white and muted sand so dense marketplace information remains readable without harsh contrast.

## Type

- Display: `Fraunces` for prices, page titles, category marks, and headline moments. It adds a market-signboard feel without becoming decorative.
- Body: `Inter` for descriptions, forms, chat, and controls, keeping repeated daily workflows clean and legible.
- Utility: `Inter` monospace fallback for compact timestamps, chips, and counters where scan speed matters.

## Signature

Listing prices use a marigold "ticket" treatment that appears on cards, details, and review states. The app should be remembered by how quickly a buyer can scan value first, then condition, distance, and freshness.
